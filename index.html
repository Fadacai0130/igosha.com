Investment Strategies

Dollar-Cost Averaging

Invest a fixed amount regularly regardless of market conditions. This strategy reduces the impact of volatility and removes the need to time the market.

Buy and Hold

Purchase quality investments and hold them for the long term. This strategy capitalizes on compound growth and minimizes trading costs.

Index Fund Investing

Invest in funds that track market indices like the S&P 500. This provides broad diversification with low fees and minimal management.

Dividend Growth

Focus on stocks with a history of increasing dividends. This provides growing passive income and potential for capital appreciation.

Asset Allocation

Balance your portfolio across different asset classes based on your risk tolerance and time horizon. Rebalance periodically to maintain your target allocation.

Value Investing

Look for undervalued stocks trading below their intrinsic value. This strategy requires research but can lead to significant returns.
